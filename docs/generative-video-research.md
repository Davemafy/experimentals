# Model choice and release gates — multi-scene video restylization

Research review: 2026-10-12. The source video, movement timing and recognizable performers must remain intact while the scene changes meaningfully.

## Research comparison

| Candidate | Structural / identity control | Environment change | Resource profile | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ID-V2V](https://github.com/Eyeline-Labs/ID-V2V) | Edited first frame + VACE video condition; additional keyframes, separate relighting/restylization paths | Yes | ~96 GB checkpoint assets; authors tested 8× A100 80 GB, single-GPU CPU-offload path described but not benchmarked for this project | **First research target**, behind storage/quality gates |
| [Wan2.1 VACE 14B](https://github.com/ali-vilab/VACE) and [official ComfyUI workflow](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/video/wan/vace) | Video/mask/depth/reference conditioning | Yes | Approximately 32 GB diffusion weights plus text encoder/VAE; 40 GB GPU may need CPU offload / block swapping | Fallback for local tests if ID-V2V cannot fit |
| [Light-A-Video](https://github.com/bcmi/Light-A-Video) / [Hi-Light](https://github.com/lxrswdd/Hi-Light) | Temporal relighting and source-detail preservation | Primarily lighting | Older diffusion/torch environments and additional checkpoints | Narrower alternative for lighting only, **not** full scene replacement |
| 1.3B VACE | Lightweight conditioning, but identity/detail limits at small resolution | Sometimes | Lower VRAM cost | Development baseline, not evidence of premium quality |
| Existing-frame exposure/compositing | Very high preservation of input pixels | No substantial environmental replacement | CPU-only | Useful control condition, not the product outcome |

## What counts as proof

A render **exists** is not proof of good output. The minimum completed experiment includes:

1. Source video; the exact edited first-frame appearance anchor; a reproducible textual prompt.
2. Unambiguous relight/restyle mode and checkpoint identifiers.
3. Actual generated video, not a grading-only simulation or pre-baked preview.
4. Whole-video review for identity, performance, camera, framing, wardrobe, scene lighting, hands, feet, intersections and motion.
5. A failed or passed report with explicit human scores: identity, performance, temporal, cinematography, transformation (0–20 each).

Acceptance: at least **85/100**, identity/performance/temporal at least **17/20** individually, and no structural decoding flags.

The criterion is a proposed product gate, not an externally validated industry score.

## First controlled trial

- One original performance, 81 source-conditioned frames (approximately 3.4 seconds at 24 fps).
- Three distinct reference edits made from the **same original frame**:
  - **Golden Hour:** retain room, transform motivated natural light.
  - **Silver Screen:** retain room, recreate directional black-and-white photographic light.
  - **Neon Noir:** regenerate environment, keep the people and motion through segmentation conditioning.
- Use the author's native **720p** generation route only after verifying disk, model assets and GPU isolation.
- Complete one look before loading more scenes. If identity or performance fails, stop and inspect conditioning rather than automatically increasing resolution or inference steps.

## Cost and storage guardrails

- The released [ID-V2V implementation](https://github.com/Eyeline-Labs/ID-V2V) documents roughly **72 GB** for the main checkpoint plus approximately **24 GB** supporting weights.
- Additional room is required for environments, cache and intermediate videos. A 100 GB volume holding other model caches is unlikely to be sufficient; measure *free space*, not total volume.
- Single A100 40 GB inference is **untested here**. CPU offload can fit some configurations at a major speed cost; do not assume the published 8×80GB performance extrapolates to one GPU.
- Do not download weights while another live inference service is being judged or depends on the GPU.
- Stop on incompatible environments, missing assets, OOM, unexpected render time or weak output. No automatic retries.

## Hosted alternative

The official [fal hosted ID-V2V listing](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/id-v2v) currently shows **$0.20 per generated second**. A nominal five-second trial is approximately $1 before any ancillary fees, subject to the provider's up-to-date pricing. This avoids a local ~96 GB download but requires a separately configured API key and spending authorization. It is **not** connected in this repository.

## What remains unverified

- Runtime and VRAM on a single A100 40 GB
- Film-grade identity and motion preservation for this particular performance
- Quality of three edited reference frames (none generated by this repository)
- 5-second 1080p export; the first trial is 81 frames at 720p
- Any live service/API integration

This laboratory does not modify or deploy a separate production project.
